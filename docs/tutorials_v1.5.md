# Tutorial Videos

The bundled videos were recorded for v1.2.10 and retain their original filenames and version labels. They explain the unchanged paper-study settings and report workflow:

- [Chinese tutorial](tutorial/LEOTherm_v1.2.10_paper_tutorial_zh.mp4)

These controls are documented in `USAGE_EN.md` and `USAGE_CN.md`; they are not shown in the older videos.
