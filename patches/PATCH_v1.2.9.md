# v1.2.9 Single-value response reporting

- One scored orbit: report the peak difference in the temperature-analysis text, without a standalone chart or chapter.
- No scored orbit: retain the diagnostic explanation in text, without an empty chart.
- Two or more scored orbits: retain the per-orbit chart, original orbit identifiers and signed differences.
- Publication response diagnostics retain the energy-balance chart when the peak-timing panel is omitted. A sole chart has no redundant panel letter.
- Chinese and English output use the same selection rule. Thermal calculations and raw result arrays are unchanged.
- The v1.2.8 source snapshot was saved before editing. Export into a new directory; previous reports are not overwritten.
